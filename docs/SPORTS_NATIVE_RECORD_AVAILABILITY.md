# Native NHL records and availability freshness — v6.0.57

## Confirmed defects and repairs

`sportsData.js:parseNhlOfficialGame` discarded supplied team records, assigned every unknown native state to scheduled, and forced neutral-site flags to false. `predictionEngine.js` also treated any third record number as a tie. For modern NHL full-game winner research, W-L-OT means wins, regulation losses and overtime/shootout losses; those last results are losses rather than half wins. The [official NHL records tables](https://records.nhl.com/standings/all-time-standings) distinguish ties from OT/SO losses. Standings points percentage is a different statistic.

`teamRecordEvidence.js` now preserves strictly parsed current native records for known modern regular seasons. It counts overtime losses in the loss denominator, preserves explicit zero-game records without adding strength evidence, and retains a minimal source event plus receipt, native event/team IDs, season/period and missingness. The descriptive record fraction is **not** a calibrated win probability. Non-NHL parsing keeps its previous behavior; other providers' record formats remain a separate audit.

Records require a current pregame receipt, a recognized fresh cache state, valid scheduled native state, numeric native team/event IDs and a compatible season/period. At every inference, retained records are rechecked against the current event/start, ordered teams, season, exact record value and the original 15-minute receipt window. Changed, future, old, started or unbound data cannot borrow the record contribution. Known final/live/preseason/playoff records are not reconstructed as historical pregame records. Unknown/malformed records remain unavailable. The calendar-scale count sanity bound is not a claimed NHL season length.

Native postponement/cancellation/unknown schedule flags now remain alerts rather than scheduled/final games. Malformed or unzoned starts are rejected. Explicit neutral-site booleans are preserved and omission remains unknown. Existing home-advantage behavior for an unknown site has not been recalibrated by this change.

Two related availability defects were reproduced. A stale summary could still report team injury coverage even though it received no fresh-summary bonus. Native goalie season context could also remain usable after its initial receipt aged, or be attached to another native event without a new inference-time check.

`summaryEvidence` now distinguishes descriptive `reportedInjurySides` from currently usable `injuryCoveredSides`. The latter requires the established 15-minute fresh receipt window and acceptable chronology. This does not clear an unavailable player: explicit Out/zero-availability rows remain subject to existing conservative restrictions. A fresh report receipt is still not proof of its underlying publication time or complete medical coverage.

`nhlGoalieFreshness.js`, `nhlGoalieResearch.js`, `inputChronology.js` and `evidenceCoverage.js` now share pregame receipt and native event/team binding checks. Both sides' numeric native IDs and abbreviations must agree; postponed/unknown event states, wrong teams, old/future receipts and unverified cache states are rejected. Retained source identity is checked again at inference and direct evidence reporting. Expired/unbound candidates no longer appear as usable season context. These are local assertion checks, not source authentication. They add no confirmed starter, health clearance or model-confidence bonus.
